Indulgence

بقيتُ في حضرتك رغم صمتي أقول الكثير

I remained in your presence; despite my silence, I say so much.

حديث · Conversation

This work explores the language of presence — how being with someone can be more eloquent than anything spoken, and how silence between people who understand each other is its own form of conversation.

The composition is quiet and intentional. The script does not demand attention — it earns it. The visual field around the text creates the feeling of a room where something important has just been understood without being said.

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
