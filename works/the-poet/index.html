New Works

عيناك حتى في صمتها لا تشكو الصيام

Your eyes — even in their silence — do not complain of the fast.

الشاعر · The Poet

There is a kind of longing that does not ask to be seen. It holds itself — eyes that carry absence without naming it, that fast from expression the way devotion fasts from noise. This line came to the artist as something already known, something recognised rather than invented. It became this print.

The calligraphy moves freely across a warm amber and linen ground — gestural, liquid, dark ink threaded with gold. The script does not sit still. It breathes. The texture beneath it feels almost worn, like paper that has been held before, like a page that belongs to someone. The composition has the intimacy of something written in private and the presence of something that refuses to stay there.

Enquire about this work

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
