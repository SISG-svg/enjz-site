Hors Série

بعضنا للوطن

Some of us are for the homeland.

النشيد المظلم · The Dark Anthem

This work explores loyalty, betrayal, and the painful recognition that not everyone who claims a country truly serves it.

The composition is heavy and deliberate. The script presses into the surface rather than floating above it, suggesting the weight of a truth that has been known for a long time but rarely spoken aloud. Darkness is not absence here — it is a condition the text inhabits.

Enquire about this work

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
