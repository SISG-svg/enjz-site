Lebanon

ثم أعود معلناً الحرب ولاعناً السلام

Then I return, declaring war and cursing peace.

المحارب · The Warrior

This piece comes from a poem about the particular stubbornness of someone who keeps surrendering and keeps coming back. The white flag is burned — not in triumph, but in exhaustion with surrender itself. The speaker does not return because he is strong. He returns because he cannot stop.

The form is a faceted geometric solid — a shield, a stone, a medallion — something that has absorbed impact and holds its shape anyway. The patina green surface carries the texture of oxidised metal, of things left in weather for a long time. The copper-rose edges and the gold dust swept across the ground are not decoration. They are what remains after a long war with oneself.

The script sits inside the form like something engraved — not written on the surface but pressed into it. The verse is not a caption. It is the object itself.

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
