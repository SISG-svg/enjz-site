Indulgence

أختار أن يقاسي قلبي على أن أقسو عليه

I choose for my heart to endure hardship rather than for me to be cruel to it.

اختيار · Choosing

This work explores the ethics of emotional life — the conscious decision to remain open rather than protective, to accept vulnerability as a form of integrity.

The composition reflects the clarity of a decision made. The script is direct and unambiguous — not tentative, not decorative. The surrounding field gives the text room to breathe and to be heard, as a choice deserves.

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
