Indulgence

كانت وردة وكنتِ اللوحة وكنتُ أنا الرسّام

There was a rose, you were the canvas and I was the painter.

ثلاثة أجزاء · The Tryptich

This work explores the nature of creation within love — how the beloved becomes both subject and surface, and how making something beautiful is inseparable from loving someone.

As a triptych, this work unfolds across three registers — three moments, three perspectives, or three stages of the same experience. Each section is complete in itself while remaining part of a larger whole. The script moves differently across each panel, reflecting the shifting nature of what is being described.

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
