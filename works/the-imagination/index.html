Indulgence

لنا على صهوة العشق موعدٌ ولقاء

Upon the saddle of love, we have a meeting and an encounter.

الخيال · The Imagination

This work explores love as movement and destination — the imagination as the only terrain where certain meetings become possible.

The script moves with a quality of forward momentum, as though the letters themselves are in transit. The surrounding composition is open and suggestive — a landscape that could be internal or external, real or conjured.

Enquire about this work

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
