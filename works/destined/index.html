Hors Série

أنِر دربَكَ وامضِ

Illuminate your path — and keep moving.

مكتوب · Destined

This is not a line from a poem. It is a phrase the artist wrote for himself, carried long enough that it became permanent — it lives on his shoulder now, tattooed into skin. This print is the same phrase given space on paper, surrounded by the energy that made it worth keeping.

The composition layers two registers of Arabic script: the central verse glows white against the dark, neon-like, lifted from the surface as though lit from within. Around and behind it, a second layer of calligraphy — a different phrase, its meaning kept to the artist alone. It is not hidden to create mystique. It is hidden because some words were never meant to be translated.

The background moves through rose, amber, gold, and deep magenta — raw, textured, alive. The dark border holds it all, the way a frame holds something that doesn't quite fit inside it.

Enquire about this work

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
