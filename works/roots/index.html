Lebanon

الوطن ساكن فيِّ. الوطن هو إنت

Home lives within me. Home is you.

جذور · Roots

This work captures the essence of how our sense of belonging and attachment to a homeland can be embodied in a single person, who then becomes a living archive of cherished memories and years of experience. It speaks to the universal experience of love and the way it can transcend geographical boundaries, making a loved one a surrogate for an entire nation and a lifetime of recollections. It evokes feelings associated with homeland, love, and even the poignant theme of exile.

Enquire about this work

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
