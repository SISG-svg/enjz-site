Indulgence

سنتين وقارّتين ومئات الكؤوس وذكرى هاتيك القُبَل

Two years, two continents, hundreds of glasses, and the memory of those kisses.

ثمل · Drunkenness

This work explores the intoxication of memory — how time and distance do not diminish desire but distil it into something more concentrated and more dangerous.

The composition accumulates rather than resolves. Elements build on one another — layers of script, layers of colour, layers of time — until the surface carries the weight of everything that has not been forgotten. The script is not decorative here; it is a record.

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
