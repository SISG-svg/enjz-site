Anyone but you.

غيرِك · Text Message

This piece begins where a poem ends. A text message written in Lebanese Arabic — the kind that arrives at an ordinary hour and says something that cannot be unsaid. By the end of it, one line holds everything: the eyes, the problem is, don't see anyone but you. The word is not a declaration. It is a diagnosis. The person writing it has noticed something about themselves — that the world has quietly rearranged itself around one person, without asking.

The calligraphy opens outward, heavy at the centre and dissolving at the edges, as if the word is still being released. Orange marks interrupt the black — not decoration, but punctuation that insists. Beneath the main script, a ghost of the same word turns in the linen ground — echo, or memory, or the thought before it became language. The composition has the feeling of something said too loudly in a quiet room.

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
