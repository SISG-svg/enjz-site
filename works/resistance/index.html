Resistance - مقاومة · Resistance

This work is taken from a poem of resistance — not against a person, but against surrender itself. The speaker refuses the posture of the wounded, refuses to accept absence as a final condition, and instead imagines storming every fortress of the beloved, gathering an army of all those undone by a single glance. The resistance is not to push her away. It is to make her return.

The composition holds that tension visibly. Two forces — black and gold — move through each other without resolution, neither consuming the other. The parchment ground carries the texture of something written and rewritten, layered with the pressure of words that refused to stay silent. The flashes of red embedded in the dark forms are not decorative. They are the cost of not surrendering.

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
