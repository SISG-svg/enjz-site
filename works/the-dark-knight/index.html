Indulgence

تخرجين من قلبي الهوينة لساعات وبمجهودٍ لأيامِ ثم أعود حارقاً رايتي البيضاء معلناً الحرب ولاعناً السلام

You leave my heart slowly, for hours, and with effort, for days — then I return burning my white flag, declaring war and cursing peace.

الفارس الأسود · The Dark Knight

This work explores the impossible cycles of love — the surrenders and the revolts, the truces that collapse, the wars that are really a form of devotion.

The script carries the full weight of contradiction: it is both surrender and declaration. The visual tension between dark and light passages mirrors the emotional push and pull of the text — two forces that cannot coexist and cannot separate.

Each work is an original giclée print on Hahnemühle archival fine art paper, signed and numbered by the artist. Editions are limited and run from A4 through A0, with the larger formats held in the smallest editions.

Nothing is held in stock. Each piece is produced after the conversation, which takes two to three weeks from the moment the edition and format are agreed. Works are supplied unframed. If a piece arrives damaged, it is replaced.
